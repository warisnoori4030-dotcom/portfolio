01 About

A structured approach to messy problems.

I'm an Engineering student at the Beijing Institute of Technology, driven by a fascination with how structured thinking and data turn messy real-world problems into clean, workable solutions.

My work sits at the intersection of analytical rigor and practical build-out, from modeling and data analysis to managing projects end-to-end. I care about precision, reproducibility, and the small details that make a system reliable rather than merely functional.

Outside the lab, you'll find me behind a camera lens or on the football pitch. Both, in their own way, are exercises in timing, composition, and reading the field.
